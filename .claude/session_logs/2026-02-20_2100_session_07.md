### Prototype 7: Bridge Scene (main work this session)
Built from a detailed brief co-designed with Claude Desktop. This is the first "scene flow" prototype — testing the pattern of narrative visuals → integrated interaction → emotional resolution.

**Concept:** Father and daughter watch a bridge being built from behind a fence. Player taps bridge sections to complete it. Camera gradually pans down to reveal the characters.

**Implementation:**
- Comic-panel framing: 1000×1750 clipped panel with dark border and drop shadow, off-white background
- 8 visual layers with parallax (sky 0.0, landscape 0.3, construction 0.6, bridge 0.5, fence/characters 1.0)
- 4 tappable bridge sections: ghost (outline) → built (solid) on tap
- Speech bubbles alternating left/right (father/daughter), with section color thumbnails
- Camera pan: SceneContainer Y offset + parallax counter-offsets per stage
- 5 daylight tint stages: dawn → morning → midday → afternoon → golden hour
- Daughter silhouette fades in during stage 2 pan
- Final stage: zoom out (0.92), standing silhouettes swap to kneeling family pose
- Advance arrow with breathing pulse animation
- All stage config (tints, pan, bubble sides, section colors) in JSON
- Asset specification document for illustrator
